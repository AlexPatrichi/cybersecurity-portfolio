# 🧪 Cooling System Maintenance & Thermal Paste Replacement
Lab Type: Real-World System Maintenance  
Difficulty: Intermediate  

## 🎯 Objective
- Perform maintenance on the HP OMEN cooling system.
- Replace the aging thermal paste on the CPU and GPU.
- Clean dust from the cooling fans and heatsink.
- Monitor CPU and GPU temperatures after reassembly to verify cooling performance.

## 🖥️ Environment
**Device**
- HP OMEN Laptop

**Operating System**
- Windows 11 Home

**Processor**
- Intel Core i7-8750H
- 6 Cores / 12 Threads

**Graphics**
- NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060

**Memory**
- 16 GB DDR4 2666 MHz
- Dual Channel

## 📋 Investigation
The laptop had been in use for several years without a thermal paste replacement.

After opening the laptop, dust accumulation was found in the cooling system, particularly around one of the fans and heatsink airflow paths.

The cooling assembly was removed to inspect and service the CPU and GPU thermal interfaces.

## 🔧 Hardware Maintenance
### Cooling System
The laptop uses a shared heat-pipe cooling assembly connected to the CPU and dedicated GPU.

Maintenance included:
- Removing the cooling assembly.
- Cleaning accumulated dust from the fans and heatsink.
- Cleaning the old thermal paste from the CPU, GPU, and heatsink contact surfaces.
- Applying fresh thermal paste to the CPU and GPU.
- Reinstalling the heatsink and tightening the mounting screws evenly.

## 🔍 Verification
After reassembly, the laptop booted normally and both CPU and GPU operation were monitored using HWiNFO.  

The laptop remained stable during normal use and gaming following the maintenance.

## 🧰 Tools Used
- Screwdriver set
- Cleaning materials
- Isopropyl alcohol
- Thermal paste MX-7
- HWiNFO

## 💡 Lessons Learned
- Cooling performance depends on the condition of the entire cooling system, not only the thermal paste.
- Thermal paste helps provide effective heat transfer between the CPU/GPU and the cooling assembly.
- Even mounting pressure is important for maintaining proper contact between the heatsink and processor surfaces.
- Monitoring temperatures after reassembly helps verify that the cooling system is operating correctly.
