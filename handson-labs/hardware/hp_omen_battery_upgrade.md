# 🧪 Battery Upgrade   
Lab Type: Real-World System Maintenance    
Difficulty: Beginner / Intermediate  

## 🎯 Objective
- Replace the aging battery in my HP OMEN laptop with a compatible replacement.
- Verify battery compatibility and confirm correct charging and stable operation after installation.

## 🖥️ Environment
**Device**
- HP OMEN Laptop

**Operating System**
- Windows 11 Home

**Processor**
- Intel Core i7-8750H
- 6 Cores / 12 Threads

**Graphics**
- NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060

**Memory**
- 8 GB DDR4 2666 MHz
- Single Channel

## 📋 Investigation
The original battery had degraded after several years of use and was no longer performing as expected.

Since the exact original HP battery was difficult to find, I compared the specifications and selected a compatible FM08 replacement with the same **14.4 V** rating.

The replacement has a slightly higher capacity of **86 Wh instead of 82 Wh**.

## 🔧 Hardware Replacement
### Original Battery
The laptop originally used an HP FM08 rechargeable lithium-ion battery.

| Property         | Original Battery |
| ---------------- | ---------------- |
| Model            | FM08             |
| HP Part Number   | 922753-421       |
| HP Spare         | 922977-855       |
| Regulatory Model | HSTNN-LB8B       |

| Capacity         | 5675 mAh         |
| Energy           | 82 Wh            |
| Chemistry        | Li-ion           |
| Date             | 2018/12          |

### Replacement Battery
A compatible FM08 replacement battery was selected based on the specifications of the original battery.

| Property         | Replacement Battery |
| ---------------- | ------------------- |
| Model            | FM08                |
| HP Spare         | 816609-005          |
| Regulatory Model | HSTNN-LB8A          |
| Voltage          | 14.4 V              |
| Capacity         | 5973 mAh            |
| Energy           | 86 Wh               |
| Chemistry        | Li-ion              |
| Label Date       | 2024/09             |

## 🔍 Compatibility & Verification
Both batteries use the same **14.4 V nominal voltage**, while the replacement provides a slightly higher capacity of **86 Wh compared with 82 Wh**.

The higher Wh rating represents additional energy capacity and does not change the voltage supplied to the laptop.

After several months of regular use, the replacement battery continues to charge and operate normally, with no stability issues observed.

## 🧰 Tools Used
- Screwdriver set
- Windows battery monitoring
- Visual inspection of battery specifications and connectors

## 💡 Lessons Learned
- The original battery specifications can be used to identify a suitable replacement when the exact part is unavailable.
- Battery **voltage and capacity are different specifications** — the voltage needs to be compatible, while a slightly higher Wh capacity can provide additional runtime.
- Successful installation is only the first check; several months of normal charging and use provided better confirmation that the replacement was reliable.
